FERC approved Cheniere's three train Sabine Pass LNG expansion in Louisiana on Thursday, about 20 million tonnes a year of new capacity, plus the Creole Trail and Sabine Crossing feed pipelines.

Across the chain: a full buildout could need 2.5 to 2.7 Bcf/d more feed gas, Phase 1 is already contracted, and the investment decision is the next step.
