A second Saudi refinery has been targeted in two days. A projectile struck Aramco's Jazan plant on the Red Sea coast on Thursday, killing a worker and halting some units, a day after the Houthis claimed attacks on Ras Tanura.

Jazan sits on the side of the kingdom Saudi exports would lean on if Gulf terminals were shut.

Who feels it: shippers and buyers already planning around Hormuz.
