US midstream companies have proposed 46.5 Bcf/d of new natural gas pipeline capacity in 2026, according to Platts, with about 20 Bcf/d starting in Texas. LNG, power plants and data centers are the demand case.

Kinder Morgan, Williams, Energy Transfer and the Solitude partners are among the builders. Most of it is due between 2028 and 2030.
