Antalya, two days past the deadline: COP31 ended Sunday without a text all countries would adopt. The fossil fuel language was the breaking point. Producer states reportedly pushed it out, and the EU refused to sign without it.

For oil and gas, the older pledge to transition away from fossil fuels still stands, with nothing new on top.
