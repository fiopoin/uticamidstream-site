Two Ohio villages, 2,150 MW of new gas-fired power, all of it for data centers. On November 19 the state siting board permitted an 800 MW plant in Ashville and a 1,350 MW plant in Millersport.

Rockies Express is lining up the gas, with binding bids due Tuesday on a lateral of up to 175,000 Dth/d near Ashville.
