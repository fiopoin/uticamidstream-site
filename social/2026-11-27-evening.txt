Suriname, so far known for oil hopes, is getting gas first. Petronas and Staatsolie took a final investment decision on KrakaWi, a $1.6 billion floating LNG project on the Sloanea field in Block 52.

It should make up to 1.5 million tonnes a year from mid 2030. Small, but it adds another Atlantic supplier next to Argentina's plans.
