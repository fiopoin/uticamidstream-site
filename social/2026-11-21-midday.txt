Diamondback Energy and KKR agreed to buy Five Point Infrastructure's 70% stake in the Deep Blue water network in the Midland basin, a deal Reuters put at about $2.1 billion. The system handles nearly 2 million barrels of water a day.

Produced water is now a core Permian midstream asset.
