The IEA's World Energy Outlook says Gulf producers could spend about $40 billion on pipelines around the Strait of Hormuz and still send about 2 million barrels a day through it by 2035. Qatari and Emirati LNG has no feasible route around the strait.

The agency also warns of a buyers' market once the crisis passes.
