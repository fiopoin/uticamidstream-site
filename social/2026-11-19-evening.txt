The IEA says global oil inventories rose by 1.7 million barrels a day in October, the first surplus since the Iran war began. Supply is up 5.3 million b/d since August, though Gulf exports remain 26% below prewar levels.

That is the backdrop as OPEC+ meets on November 29 to set its 2027 plan.
