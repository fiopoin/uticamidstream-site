Korea now owns a slice of the Marcellus. POSCO International closed its $550 million purchase of Chord Energy's Pennsylvania gas stake on Monday.

The position produced about 121 MMcf/d over the past year. A local operator keeps the wells running and POSCO sells the gas, with some headed to LNG plants from 2029. Chord walks away to focus on the Williston.
