How we work

Utica Midstream is a free daily edition on oil and gas from wellhead to market. It covers upstream, midstream and downstream; onshore and offshore; conventional and unconventional; and the policy, capital and cross-industry work, such as carbon capture and produced water, that touches all of them.

Prices

The board shows the latest quotes available when the edition is built, with the time and the source on every row. Futures quotes move all day, so treat them as a snapshot. Spreads are calculated from the quotes shown and labeled as calculated.

Markets and stocks

Index levels are the prior session's close. Stocks appear when they are in the news; we show a price only when the source gives a dated close.

Along the chain

Every lead story is traced through upstream, midstream, downstream and the market, because a price move or a permit rarely stops at one link.

The docket

Decisions by regulators, governments and producer groups such as OPEC+, with the date, the status reported by the agency and a link to the filing or report.

Leader of the week

Once a week we profile a person whose decisions are moving the industry, from public statements and filings. Profiles are not endorsements.

Standards

Every fact is linked to a dated source. We paraphrase and quote sparingly. Outlook lines are our reading and are labeled. Corrections are made in the archive. This is general information, not investment, legal or tax advice.
